Weeknight lentil soup

Ingredients: one onion, two carrots, two celery stalks, three cloves of
garlic, one cup of red lentils, one can of diced tomatoes, four cups of
vegetable broth, one teaspoon of cumin, salt and pepper to taste.

Method: dice the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook them in olive oil over
medium heat until the onion turns soft and translucent, about six
minutes. Add the garlic and cumin and cook for another minute.

Stir in the lentils, tomatoes, and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce
the heat and simmer for twenty five minutes, until the lentils are
tender. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with crusty bread.

Notes: this soup keeps well in the fridge for about four days and
freezes even better. Swap the broth for water and add more cumin if you
want a deeper flavor.
